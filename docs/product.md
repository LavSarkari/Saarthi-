# Product Documentation

## Overview
Saarthi is an AI Execution Operating System designed for ambitious knowledge workers. It shifts the burden of planning, risk calculation, and recovery from the human to the AI. Traditional software optimizes planning; Saarthi optimizes execution.

## The Five Hero Capabilities
- **Brain Dump**: Capture chaotic, unstructured intentions via text, voice, or image, and transform them into structured commitments.
- **Adaptive AI Planning**: Autonomously decompose massive goals into realistic, minute-by-minute execution plans.
- **Recovery OS**: When life goes wrong, generate a Compromise Strategy to salvage the deadline and rebuild the week.
- **Execution Activation Engine**: Detect execution paralysis and generate tiny, frictionless starting points to build immediate momentum.
- **Behavioral Intelligence**: Continuously learn the user's focus patterns, completion rates, and fatigue levels to optimize future interventions.

## Supporting Ecosystem
- **Predictive Risk Engine:** Continuously computes the mathematical probability of completion in real-time.
- **Continuous Voice Coaching:** Low-latency, real-time voice consultations with Gemini Live WebSockets for spoken execution reflections.
- **Calendar & Task Synchronization:** Seamlessly provisions extracted milestones to Google Calendar.
- **Telegram Companion:** Delivers critical alerts and daily briefings directly to the user's phone.

## Execution Journey
1. **Onboarding**: The user establishes their psychological execution profile (Companion) and connects Google Calendar.
2. **Ingestion**: The user enters a vague objective or uploads a syllabus photo for Gemini Vision extraction.
3. **Decomposition**: Saarthi breaks down the objective into micro-steps with exact minute allotments.
4. **Execution & Activation**: The user works through the steps. If paralysis is detected, the Activation Engine intervenes.
5. **Continuous Recovery**: The Completion Confidence Engine tracks progress. If the deadline becomes at-risk, Saarthi triggers a Compromise Recovery Plan.
